ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. WHERE CLAUSE DOES NOT EXCLUDE is_complete=1

The enrichment scripts (INGEST-MOVIES-LOGIC.js and INGEST-SERIES-LOGIC.js) 
have a WHERE clause that DOES NOT exclude already-complete items:

WHERE (
  (overview_en IS NULL AND is_filtered = 0)
  OR (overview_en IS NOT NULL AND (title_ar = 'TBD' OR title_ar IS NULL))
  OR (overview_en IS NOT NULL
      AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM cast_crew WHERE content_tmdb_id = movies.tmdb_id AND content_type = 'movie'))
)

The console message "العناصر المكتملة (is_complete=1) مش هتتفحص تاني هنا" is FALSE.

2. PROOF FROM DATABASE

- 36 movies that are STILL complete were updated during enrichment (2026-08-16 07:00-08:30)
- 42 series that are STILL complete were updated during enrichment
- 50 movies that became incomplete during enrichment
- 50 series that became incomplete during enrichment

3. WHY ITEMS BECAME INCOMPLETE

All verified "should be complete" items (1737162, 1739346, 586950, 1217681, 1360415) 
are actually MISSING CAST in the database. TMDB does not provide cast data for:
- Documentaries
- Educational videos
- Music videos
- Web series
- TV specials
- Some low-budget content

Similarly, series like 258411, 235023, 123503, 84229 are missing BOTH cast AND genres.

4. THE is_complete CALCULATION IS CORRECT

Movies require: title_ar, title_en, overview_ar, poster_path, cast, genres
Series require: title_ar, title_en, overview_ar, poster_path, cast, genres, seasons

The items that lost is_complete=1 genuinely don't meet these requirements.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT HAPPENED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Some items were marked is_complete=1 in the past despite missing cast/genres
   (possibly from an earlier version of the enrichment logic with looser validation)

2. The enrichment scripts re-processed these items during this session because:
   - The WHERE clause matched them (missing cast in cast_crew table)
   - They had is_complete=1 but no cast in the database

3. The enrichment scripts fetched fresh data from TMDB, which STILL had no cast/genres

4. The is_complete calculation correctly evaluated to 0 (missing cast/genres)

5. The items were updated with is_complete=0, causing the observed drop

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CONCLUSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The drop is NOT a bug. It's a CORRECTION of previously-incorrect is_complete=1 values.

The items that lost is_complete=1 genuinely don't have the required data in TMDB.

The WHERE clause in enrichment scripts allows re-processing of already-complete items,
which in this case caught and corrected items that shouldn't have been marked complete.

If you want to prevent re-processing already-complete items in the future, add this
to the WHERE clause:

  AND is_complete = 0

But be aware this will prevent correcting any future data quality issues in
already-complete items.
